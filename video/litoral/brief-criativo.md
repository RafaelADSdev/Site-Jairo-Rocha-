# Creative brief — Viva o litoral

## Purpose and claim boundary

Invite a visitor to explore South Pernambuco destinations and discuss a possible seasonal stay. This is an editorial inspiration film, not a list of confirmed rental inventory. The only location names appear as destination choices; no routes, times, rates, availability, property features or investment return are represented as verified facts.

## Audience and concept

- Audience: couples and families considering a coastal break.
- Desire: change the pace of daily life and choose a trip that matches their group.
- Visual thesis: cinematic editorial photography with large, left-aligned Manrope text; deep burgundy overlay for clear reading, coral chapter accents and generous margins. Slow camera crops let the images breathe. A single progressive line connects destination choices without imitating a real geographic map.
- Primary message: “Seu próximo capítulo tem mar.”
- CTA: explore destinations and confirm dates, accommodation and conditions with the team.
- Evidence distinction: actual photographs represent the named beach destinations only. They do not document available rental inventory or property amenities. An early AI-photo draft was explicitly superseded by the user's request for real local beaches and is excluded from the final film.

## Reproducible tokens

- Burgundy field: `#651d32`.
- Coral accents: `#ffad94`.
- Warm white text: `#fffdfc`.
- Type: existing local Manrope font, 400/600/700; 59–82 px headlines depending on composition, 20–27 px supporting text.
- Margins: 68 px in 1280 × 720 landscape; 50 px in 720 × 960 portrait.
- Transition: 15-frame crossfade; initial text movement 24 px over 26 frames.
- Camera: slow, limited scale/translation of the original still image; no invented motion or synthetic scene morphing.
- Signature: plain brand name, not a reconstructed logo.
- Web playback intent: user-initiated playback, visible controls, no automatic audio. Portrait is an independent arrangement, not automatic cropping of the landscape composition.

## Source and permission

The user initially requested ImageGen and a Remotion experience, then explicitly required real photographs of nearby listed beaches. The latest request supersedes the early draft. The parent task sources four real destination photographs and records authors/licensing with the page assets. The film does not assert that these images document any available rental listing.

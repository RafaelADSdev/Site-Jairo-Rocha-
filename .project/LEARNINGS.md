## 2026-09-13 — Investor research

- Separate original asking prices, calendar quotes, provider estimates and audited owner statements; do not turn one category into another.
- Muro Alto/Porto share Ipojuca; Carneiros/urban Tamandaré share Tamandaré. De-duplicate by actual location/property, not portal label.
- Public STR providers can disagree materially; do not average incompatible populations or select the higher return. Currency and availability denominators must remain explicit.
- Operating income is not owner after-tax cash flow. Include total invested capital, fixed costs, commissions on cleaning and impossible break-even states.
- Deep Research plus independent purchase/source review produced a shareable dossier without mutating the public simulator or promising returns.
